Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Claude Skin Switcher (the desktop app and its public skin gallery) is run by Soso Technologies LLC.

Browsing and installing skins

No account is needed. To limit abuse, the gallery counts downloads and reports per network address using a salted one-way hash; the address itself is not stored.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is only needed to upload skins. The app asks Google for the openid scope only, so Google tells us an account identifier and nothing else: not your email address, name or photo. The gallery stores a salted one-way hash of that identifier to recognise you, plus the public name you choose. Your sign-in stays encrypted on your own computer.

What is public

Skins you upload, the public name you chose, and upload dates are public. You can delete your skins at any time from the app.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data. The gallery runs on Cloudflare and this website on GitHub Pages; both process requests to serve them. The website loads gallery data from Cloudflare and release information from GitHub in your browser.
